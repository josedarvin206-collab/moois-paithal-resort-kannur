Good to know

Before you visit.

How can I check room availability and rates?

Send your intended dates and guest count through the enquiry form or WhatsApp. The resort must confirm availability, rates and inclusions directly.

Can I enquire about group stays or events?

Yes. Mention your group size and event requirements in the message. Ask the team to confirm available spaces, capacity, pricing and any conditions.

Are activities and facilities always available?

Not necessarily. Activities, food services and facilities may depend on the date, weather, operational status and additional charges. Confirm details before travelling.

Does submitting this form confirm my booking?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp message. Your reservation is confirmed only after the resort responds and agrees to the booking details and terms.
